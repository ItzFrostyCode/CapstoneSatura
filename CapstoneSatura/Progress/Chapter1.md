INTRODUCTION
Project Context
The industry of tailoring and garment customization has a rich heritage in
traditional skills, offering vital services ranging from tailored everyday clothing
to specialized uniforms and formal wear. Even with the increasing demand for
personalized garments, numerous local tailoring shops and boutiques still rely
on outdated, paper-based practices. This dependence on manual record- keeping creates a considerable obstacle in their daily functioning. Customer
information, detailed body measurements, and specific design preferences
are frequently noted in physical logbooks, which makes them vulnerable to
loss, damage, or misinterpretation. When a physical record is compromised,
the artisan loses more than just data; they lose the technical blueprint
required to deliver a product that meets the specific anatomical needs of the
client. This reliance on physical media represents a significant point of failure
in a way where data integrity is paramount to business continuity and
professional service delivery. Moreover, the operational intricacies of a tailoring enterprise go beyond
managing customers; they rely significantly on a consistent and effectively
monitored supply chain. Relying on manual inventory tracking often results in
inconsistencies between the physical stock and the recorded inventory. This
lack of efficiency complicates shop owners' ability to track essential materials
like certain fabrics, threads, and hardware, causing unexpected shortages, delays in procurement from suppliers, and ultimately, interruptions in
production processes. As a result, these operational challenges contribute to
postponed job orders, reduced customer satisfaction, and restricted capacity
for business growth. There is an urgent need for a digital overhaul in this
industry to close the divide between customer-facing services and back-end
supply chain operations. On the other hand, digital transformation in the manufacturing sector is
becoming the foundation of an extensive array of highly efficient management
and personal verification solutions. Today, centralized web-based systems
are being spotlighted because of the need for reliable data security and real￾time operational visibility. Many applications in everyday commerce require
accurate tracking, including inventory control, client profiling, and supplier
logistics. While these technologies are widely used in general retail, managing
the specific complexities of bespoke tailoring remains a challenge that
requires a dedicated focus. A structured management system plays a key role
in the success of any small enterprise, providing incentives for productivity
and assisting in identifying problem areas. It is the most vital means of
analyzing performance, where accuracy in client specifications is needed for
high-quality garment construction. A series of problems arises due to manual processing of both customer
orders and supply chain management. Through conducting interviews in local
boutiques and observing their day-to-day operations, we have noted that the
peak season for uniforms and formal wear often leads to a state of
administrative paralysis. The sheer volume of loose papers, fabric swatches
pinned to sticky notes, and unorganized supplier receipts creates a chaotic
environment where errors are not just possible, but inevitable. We have
witnessed firsthand the frustration of shop owners who realize a critical
material is missing only after the cutting process has begun, leading to costly
delays and customer disappointment. These experiences highlight a
prevailing problem situation where the lack of a centralized data repository
directly hampers the creative and productive capacity of the tailor,
transforming what should be a precise art into a stressful management
struggle.
In sight of these problems, the researchers proposed Sutura: A Web-based
Subscription Tailoring Business Management System with Supplier
Management. This system provides a scalable solution by utilizing a
subscription-based model, which is specifically designed to support small-to- medium enterprises (SMEs). Unlike traditional software that requires a heavy
upfront investment, a subscription model allows local shop owners to access
high-end management tools through an affordable monthly or yearly fee. This
approach ensures that the business remains financially agile while benefiting
from continuous cloud updates, remote data backups, and technical support. Sutura will provide digitized storage of detailed body measurements and
specific design preferences, making the misinterpretation of client needs
impossible. It will also have a backend supply chain module provided for boutique
owners in order to expedite their needs for more efficient and faster tracking of fabrics
and hardware. As researchers, we recognize that the solution to this problem is not merely a digital
version of a logbook, but a comprehensive system that synchronizes client data with
supplier logistics. Our technical proficiency allows for the creation of a specialized
platform that caters specifically to the nuances of tailoring such as multi-point
measurement tracking and incremental fitting schedules which generic business
software often ignores. This research is a direct application of technological
intervention to preserve and enhance a traditional craft that is currently being stifled
by administrative inefficiency. The relationship between the rationale of this study and the proposed research
problem is centered on the necessity of bridging the technological gap to ensure long￾term business sustainability and operational excellence. The rationale posits that the
current manual system is the primary bottleneck preventing growth, precision, and
customer satisfaction in the tailoring sector. Consequently, the proposed research
serves as the direct technological response to these documented failures, aiming to
harmonize front-end client customization with back-end inventory and supplier logistics
into one cohesive, scalable digital ecosystem. Purpose and Description
The primary purpose of the Sutura project is to provide a comprehensive, cloud-enabled
digital platform that simplifies the daily operations of tailoring shops and fashion
designers. Moving away from vulnerable paper-based records, the system serves as a
centralized space to track the entire garment production lifecycle from initial customer
measurements to final delivery. By utilizing a subscription-based model, the project
aims to make professional-grade management tools accessible and affordable for
small-to-medium enterprises (SMEs), removing the high upfront costs of traditional
software while ensuring continuous data security and updates.
The project functions as an all-in-one assistant that automates the administrative work.
It allows tailors to store exact body measurements and design preferences digitally, ensuring precision in every order. Simultaneously, the integrated supply chain module
monitors the stock of fabrics and hardware in real-time. When materials run low, the
system proactively alerts the owner and facilitates seamless communication with
registered suppliers. This innovative approach ensures that Staffs can focus on their
creative craft rather than manual paperwork and inventory struggles. Listed below are the specific purposes of the study:
Centralized Digital Records: To allow business owners and staff to instantly record and
retrieve detailed customer measurements and design preferences through a secure
subscription portal.  To provide automated tracking of fabrics and hardware with low-stock alerts to
prevent production delays.  To enable direct management and communication with material suppliers to ensure
a steady and reliable supply chain.  To monitor the real-time status of every garment in production, from the initial
cutting phase to the final fitting.  To offer a platform where freelance designers can manage personal portfolios and
showcase products as part of their subscription features. Objectives
General Objective
To design and develop a cloud-based subscription tailoring business management
system with an integrated supply chain module that centralizes customer management,
job order tracking, inventory monitoring, supplier and procurement recording, billing, and
reporting to improve operational efficiency and business monitoring.

Specific Objectives

1. Business Registration, Subscription, and User Management
   To allow tailoring businesses to register, subscribe, and securely access the system
   using role-based user accounts. To enable fashion designers to subscribe to the
   system, manage their profiles, create and view posts, and` publish products to
   showcase their designs.

2. Customer, Measurement, and Order Management
   To manage customer profiles, store body measurements, allow submission of
   tailoring orders, and track job order status digitally.

3. Appointment and Notification Management
   To schedule fittings, consultations, and order release dates and provide automated
   notifications for order updates and deadlines.

4. Inventory and Supplier Management
   To monitor the availability of tailoring materials, and automatically notify the shop
   when stock is low, allowing them to request or reorder materials from suppliers to
   prevent delays in production.

5. Billing and Payment Management
   To automatically generate invoices and record payments for accurate financial
   tracking.

6. Reports and Administrative Management
   To generate operational and inventory reports and provide dashboards for
   monitoring business and supply chain activities. Scope and Limitations
   Scope

The system will focus on developing a cloud-based subscription tailoring business
management system with an integrated supply chain module accessible through the
internet. It will include the following features:
• Business registration and subscription-based access
• Secure login and role-based user management • Customer profile and body measurement management • Job order creation and tracking
• Appointment scheduling and automated notifications
• Inventory monitoring and automatic stock updates
• Supplier record management and procurement tracking
• Billing, invoice generation, and payment recording
• Reports and dashboard for monitoring business and supply chain activities. The system will be used by tailoring shop owners and authorized staff to manage
tailoring operations and supply chain processes through a centralized platform. Limitations
The proposed system will have the following limitations: • The system requires internet access to function. • Customer measurements must be entered manually by users or staff. • SMS or email notifications may not be included initially. • Online subscription payment integration may not be included in the initial version. • The system does not include hardware integration such as automated measuring
devices. • System performance may depend on internet connectivity and server availability.
