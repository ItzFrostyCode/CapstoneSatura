Sutura Production Technical Guide

Stitching Standards

  • Formal Suits 12-14 SPI
  • Silk / Satin 16-18 SPI
  • Denim / Twill 8-10 SPI
  • Seam Allowance 3/8 inch

Needle Selection

  • Fine Silk 60/8 or 70/10
  • Mid-weight Wool 80/12 or 90/14
  • Heavy Coating 100/16
  • Thread Type Polyester Core

Material Handling Guidelines

Silk & Delicate Fabrics
Always test tension on a scrap piece. Use fresh needles to avoid snags. Never pin in the main body area.
Bespoke Canvas & Interlining
Pad stitching should be invisible from the right side. Ensure hair canvas is pre-shrunk before attachment.
